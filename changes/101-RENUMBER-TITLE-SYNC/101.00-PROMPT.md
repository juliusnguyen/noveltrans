# Feature: a renumber must reach the video's title everywhere, including YouTube

## Request (original, Vietnamese)

Khi đổi số phần, tại sao không update luôn phần chi tiết video (tiêu đề)?

## Translation / summary

After "Đổi số phần…" (feature 099), the parts table shows the corrected number but the
**Chi tiết phần** dialog still shows the old one. Two separate causes, verified below.

## Verified behaviour (measured, not assumed)

| Part state | Table column | Detail dialog / `.title.txt` |
|---|---|---|
| Rendered, NOT published | `Phần 75` ✅ | `Phần 75` ✅ |
| Rendered, **published** | `Phần 75` ✅ | `Phần 1` ❌ |
| `.title.txt` exists, `.mp4` deleted | `Phần 75` ✅ | `Phần 1` ❌ |

In the reporting user's project (`~/NovelTrans/novel-b1033b5d`) **all 12 rendered parts are
published**, so row 2 is hit every time.

## Cause 1 — published parts (deliberate, but the follow-through is missing)

`tab_video._renumber_title_sidecars` skips a published part on purpose: its `.title.txt`
should keep matching what viewers actually see, because renumbering cannot rename a video
already on the channel.

The assumption was that "Cập nhật tiêu đề" would then offer the part so the user could push
the new number. It does not. `_title_update_rows` (`tab_video.py:4691`) compares the
recorded channel title against `retitle(current, …)`, and `rename.retitle` lifts the
`Phần N` group **verbatim** (`rename.py:158`) — so `fresh == current`, it returns `""`, and
the row is skipped.

Measured: `offered to 'Cập nhật tiêu đề' on YouTube: False`.

**Net effect: there is no way in the app to push a corrected part number to YouTube.**
Feature 099's plan flagged this as a possible follow-up and it was deferred.

Useful detail for the fix: the `.title.txt` is **not** the record of what is on the
channel — `upload_state["title"]` is, and `_title_update_rows:4716-4719` only falls back to
the sidecar for parts uploaded before that was recorded. So rewriting a published part's
sidecar loses no information.

## Cause 2 — a plain bug in feature 099

`_renumber_title_sidecars` guards with `if not out.is_file() or is_published(out): continue`.
The intent was "only touch rendered parts", but `_part_metadata` (`tab_video.py:2072`) reads
`.title.txt` whenever it exists, regardless of the `.mp4`. So a leftover sidecar from a
video that was deleted after upload beats the correct computed title, with nothing published
to justify keeping it. The `out.is_file()` half of the guard should go; `is_published` is
the only condition that earns its place.

## Requirements

1. Drop the `out.is_file()` condition in `_renumber_title_sidecars` — a sidecar with no
   video and no upload record has no reason to hold a stale number.
2. Rewrite `.title.txt` for **published** parts at/after the anchor too, so the detail
   dialog, the table and the sidecar all agree on the number the part should now have.
3. Make `_title_update_rows` notice a part-number change, so a renumbered published part is
   offered under "Cập nhật tiêu đề" and the user can push it to YouTube. It must keep the
   existing guards: a hand-written title is still left alone (`rename.title_name`), and
   only parts with an upload record are considered.
4. Nothing may be sent to YouTube automatically — the existing confirm-then-push flow stays
   the only way a live title changes.
5. `_confirm_renumber`'s wording needs revisiting: it currently says the YouTube title will
   NOT change and the user must fix it manually. After this change it should point at
   "Cập nhật tiêu đề" instead.

## Open questions

- Should `_title_update_rows` diff against the recomputed `_part_title(part_num)` outright,
  or should the part-number comparison be separate from the rename/order-flip comparison
  `retitle` already does? The two must not fight: a rename, an order flip and a renumber can
  all be pending on the same part at once.
- A part renumbered while published and then re-rendered gets a fresh sidecar from the
  render worker anyway. Check that the two paths agree rather than racing.
