# 083 — Sound bars: frozen on long videos, optional, and no longer overlapped by titles

Three things about the video's audio visualizer ("cột sóng nhạc"), reported in one session:

1. **The bars freeze part-way through a long video.** They animate at the start and then
   stop, while the audio and the playhead keep going to the end. Short videos look fine.
2. **Let the bars be turned off.** Follow-up question first — "if we disable the soundwave,
   will the render time reduce?" — then: add the option.
3. **A long chapter title overlaps the bars.** Some chapter names wrap to two or three
   lines and run straight over the visualizer.

## What already exists

- `tts/video.py` — `render_video` bakes three `player_skin` PNGs (skin / vinyl / knob) and
  animates them in one ffmpeg call: `[1:a]showfreqs` bars, a spun vinyl, a playhead whose
  x is `t/total`, ASS titles burned on top, `-map 1:a -c:a copy -shortest`.
- `build_ass_subtitles` — pure ASS builder; Novel + Chapter styles are Alignment 8 boxed
  into the right column by `PlayerLayout`'s L/R margins and a per-style `MarginV`.
- `PlayerLayout` (`tts/player_skin.py`) — the single source of truth for the geometry the
  skin, the filtergraph and the ASS builder must agree on.
- The Video tab already has this shape of option in `burn_subtitles`: an `AppConfig`
  property + a `WORKFLOW_KEYS` entry + a checkbox + a `VideoWorker` argument.
