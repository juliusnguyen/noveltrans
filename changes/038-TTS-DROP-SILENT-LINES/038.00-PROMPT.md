# 038 — A line of pure punctuation must be silence, not a noise

## The ask

> the audio render making the weird sound whenever the text is `...`

```
“Tên bây giờ khó nghe quá.”

“…”

Một vấn đề hoàn toàn không nên xuất hiện trong trường hợp này.
```

> the line `…` rendered by Ngọc Linh — Nữ, Bắc — phong cách tự nhiên makes a weird speech

## What's happening

`clean_for_tts` keeps `…` and the quote characters on purpose — they carry prosody inside
a sentence (`Anh ta ngập ngừng… rồi im lặng.`). That is right in general, and wrong for a
paragraph that is *only* punctuation.

The path such a line takes today:

1. `clean_for_tts` leaves `“…”` untouched — every character is in the keep-set.
2. `split_sentences` makes it its own chunk (it is its own paragraph): 3 characters.
3. `merge_short_chunks` (feature 028, `min_chunk_chars = 30`) glues it onto the
   neighbouring sentence, since a 3-char chunk is far below the floor.
4. The engine is handed `“Tên bây giờ khó nghe quá.” “…”` and vocalises the ellipsis.

So the beat the author wrote as *silence* comes out as a sound.

## Goal

A line with nothing speakable on it — `“…”`, `……`, `“?”`, `- - -` — should produce a
pause, not a noise. Vietnamese web novels use these constantly for a beat of silence in
dialogue.

## Open questions for `/plan`

- Where to fix it: `clean_for_tts` (covers the render *and* the GUI preview, which both
  call it) vs. the chunking layer.
- What "nothing speakable" means precisely — no letters and no digits, presumably, so a
  bare `1` still gets read.
- Whether the paragraph gap that replaces the dropped line is enough of a beat, or
  whether the silence needs to be lengthened to stand in for it.
- What happens to a punctuation-only line sitting *between* two lines of one paragraph
  (single newlines), where dropping it would fuse or re-split the paragraph.
- Ellipses **inside** a sentence must keep working — that is prosody, not silence, and
  breaking it would be a worse bug than the one being fixed.

## Out of scope

- Changing `min_chunk_chars` or the merge heuristic from 028.
- Anything about voice choice or engine settings.
