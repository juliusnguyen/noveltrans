### Two things worth recording

**`merge.part_number` and `plan_locked_video_windows` are untouched.** The offset is
applied strictly on top of their output, which is what makes "numbers shift, spans never
do" structural rather than a thing to remember. Same reason the offsets live in their own
file: `video_manual_windows.json` is fed straight into `plan_locked_video_windows`'s
`committed` argument, so a delta leaking in there would reshape a chapter span.
`tests/test_video.py::TestPartNumberOffsets` and
`TestRenumberParts::test_the_chapter_spans_are_untouched` pin both halves.

**Nothing on disk is renamed or deleted.** A part's file is named after its chapter range
(`video_part_name`), not its number, so unlike a split or a merge a renumber needs no
`_delete_rendered_part`. `.title.txt` is rewritten for rendered-but-unpublished parts
only (a published sidecar keeps matching the channel; a hand-written title is left alone
— the same two guards `resync_title_sidecars` applies). The `.jpg` bakes "PHẦN N" but is
deliberately not regenerated: that would discard a cover tuned in the thumbnail editor,
so the status line points at "Tạo lại tất cả ảnh bìa" instead.

### Corrected during implementation

The plan's collision rule (refuse when the new number would collide with the part before
it) turned out to block the literal example in the prompt if part 74 is itself on screen
— renumbering 75 → 74 collides. That is the correct answer (two videos of one novel
sharing a title would break `youtube_upload`'s title matching), but a bare refusal would
be a dead end, so the error now names the better anchor: *"Nếu cả Phần 74 cũng sai số,
hãy đổi số từ Phần 74 trở đi."* Wanting a part lower generally means the numbering went
wrong earlier, and renumbering from there drags the rest along.
