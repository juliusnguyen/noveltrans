# 037 — The Video tab needs to scroll

## The ask

> it lose the character, we should able to scroll here

With a screenshot of the Video tab on a 75-part project: the "Danh sách phần video" table
is squeezed to a **single, vertically clipped row**, and several boxes below it are cut
off mid-widget — the "Chế độ hiển thị" combo, the "Danh sách phát" field and the upload
buttons are all sliced through horizontally.

## What's happening

`VideoTab.__init__` stacks eight things in one `QVBoxLayout` with no scrolling:

```python
layout.addWidget(self._build_video_box())
layout.addWidget(self._build_video_list_box(), stretch=1)   # the parts table
layout.addWidget(self._build_upload_box())
layout.addWidget(self._build_thumbnail_box())
layout.addWidget(self._build_image_prompt_box())
layout.addWidget(self._build_tags_box())
layout.addWidget(self.progress)
layout.addWidget(self.status_label)
```

When the window is shorter than the content's natural height, Qt shrinks widgets below
their sizeHint. The parts table is the only one with `stretch=1`, so it collapses first
and hardest — down to one clipped row — and once it has nothing left to give, the fixed
boxes start getting clipped too. There is no `QScrollArea` anywhere in the GUI, so
nothing can scroll and the content simply has nowhere to go.

The tab has also been growing: 033 added the upload box, 035 added the "Tên hiển thị"
row. Each addition takes height from the table.

## Goal

The tab scrolls, and the parts table keeps enough height to be usable at any window size.

## Open questions for `/plan`

- Whether the progress bar and status line stay pinned outside the scroll area (they are
  live feedback during a long run) or scroll with everything else.
- The table's minimum height, and whether it keeps its own scrollbar inside a scrolling
  page — nested scrolling is confusing, so the table wants a bounded viewport rather
  than growing to fit all 75 rows.
- Whether the other tabs have the same latent problem, and whether to fix them here or
  note it.

## Out of scope

- Redesigning the tab's contents or collapsing boxes into sections.
