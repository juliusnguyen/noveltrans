# 037 — VIDEO TAB SCROLL — history

## [PLAN] 037.01-INITIAL-PLAN — 2026-07-28

Generated plan: `037.01-INITIAL-PLAN.md`.

Diagnosis: `VideoTab` stacked six group boxes in one `QVBoxLayout` with no scrolling. When
the window is shorter than the content's natural height (1072 px, measured), Qt's only
option is shrinking widgets below their sizeHint. The parts table owns the sole `stretch=1`,
so it collapsed first and hardest — to a single clipped row — and once it had nothing left
to give, the fixed boxes started getting sliced through as well.

## [IMPLEMENTATION] — 2026-07-28

927 tests pass (921 before, +6), ruff clean.

### `gui/tab_video.py`

- The six boxes moved into a content `QWidget` inside a new `QScrollArea`
  (`setWidgetResizable(True)`, `NoFrame`, horizontal `AsNeeded`).
- **Progress bar and status line pinned outside the scroll area.** A render or upload
  batch runs for minutes to hours; feedback you have to scroll to find is feedback you
  don't see, least of all while scrolled down reading the parts table.
- `video_list.setMinimumHeight(240)` — ~6 rows + header. The table keeps its own scrollbar
  rather than growing to fit all 75 parts, so a wheel gesture over it never fights the
  page's scroll.
- Horizontal policy is `AsNeeded`, deliberately not `Off`: `Off` clips silently if content
  turns out wider than the viewport, which is the exact failure this change exists to end.

### Measured, not assumed

Instantiated the tab against a real project and read the geometry back at three sizes:

| window | table | content | viewport | scrollable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1400×420 | **240 px (~7 rows)** | 1072 px | 370 px | 702 px |
| 1400×1000 | 240 px | 1072 px | 950 px | 122 px |
| 1400×1600 | **718 px** | 1550 px | 1550 px | 0 |

Two things this confirms that the code alone doesn't: the collapse is gone at the size that
broke, and the minimum is a *floor* rather than a fixed size — once the viewport exceeds the
content's natural height the stretch hands the spare space back to the table.

### The regression test earns its place

`test_the_parts_table_survives_a_short_window` was verified by deleting
`setMinimumHeight(240)` and re-running: it fails, and passes again once restored. Without
that check it would have been a test that merely described the new behaviour rather than
one that defends it.

### Not done, deliberately

The other five tabs share the same no-scroll-area pattern and could grow into the same
problem. Only the Video tab is fixed here: it is the one with six group boxes and the one
in the report, and changing five tabs to fix one reported bug is scope that wasn't asked
for. Worth a follow-up if any other tab starts clipping.

### Known trade-off

Nested scrolling — the wheel over the parts table scrolls the table, elsewhere it scrolls
the page. Standard behaviour, and the alternative (a 75-row table inside a scrolling page)
is worse.
