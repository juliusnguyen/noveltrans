`_start_subtitles` read `self.config.tts_gap`; the property is `tts_gap_seconds`. It raised
`AttributeError` the moment the button was clicked.

## [FIX 2] The silence threshold was wrong, so 49 of 51 parts were skipped

The button *did* work — two `.srt` files were written. Coverage was the problem: only the
part containing the one re-voiced chapter got one.

### The premise was right; the discriminator was not

Measured against a real chapter whose cues were known (77 cues → 76 true gaps):

| threshold | silences found | aligned with a true gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| −50 dB (shipped) | 98 | 67 |
| −60 dB | 77 | 76 |
| **−70 dB** | **76** | **76** |
| **−80 dB** | **76** | **76** |

At −50 dB the detector was catching quiet *speech*. The inter-chunk gaps are `np.zeros` —
true digital silence — while a natural pause is quiet but not zero, so **the threshold is
what separates them**.

### The length filter was actively harmful

042 filtered detected silences to those within ±0.25 s of the nominal gap. Measured: the
real gaps are 0.347–0.348 s exactly (`0.4 / 1.15`), but `silencedetect` reports a region
slightly *inside* the gap, because speech trails off around it. So a real gap is never
reported at its real length, and the filter kept **7 of 76**. Removed.

### Why the 042 verification missed all of this

The synthetic test audio was random noise at constant amplitude right up to the gap edge.
Real TTS trails off. The fake failed to model the single property the detector depends on,
so it reported 0.0 ms error on a scenario that does not occur. **A verification is only as
good as the fake's fidelity on the axis being verified** — and the axis here was the
*edges* of speech, which the fake had none of.

### The fix: calibrate instead of hardcoding

`find_chunk_gaps` tries thresholds quietest-first (−90 … −50 dB) and accepts the first
yielding exactly `len(chunks) - 1` silences. The expected count is known from the text, so
it is used as a **calibration signal**, not only as a safety check — which is what makes
this work across formats, speeds and voices instead of depending on a constant guessed in
advance. No threshold matching → refusal, unchanged.
